    ETHICS VS. MORALS

    Ethics vs. Morals in Ocular's Design

    Did our design follow personal convenience or professional responsibility?

    Morals — Personal Beliefs

    "I'll hardcode the API key — it saves development time."
    "This open-source model is free, so I can use it however I want."
    "Skipping tests is fine — it works on my machine."

    Ethics — Professional Standards

    "API keys must be stored in .env files — never in source code."
    "Attribute all third-party libraries correctly in requirements.txt."
    "Every external dependency must be reviewed for license compliance."
    ✓ Our Design Choice: We followed professional ethics → API key in .env, open libraries credited, no shortcuts taken.
    PROFESSIONAL ETHICS

    Three non-negotiable pillars of our development approach

    PerceptionAgent · AnalysisAgent · FormattingAgent · DocumentAgent · MemoryAgent

    Confidence score displayed — users always know result reliability

    ETHICAL DECISION MAKING

    A Real Ethical Decision We Faced

    Speed vs. Quality — Should we skip image preprocessing to make the app faster?

    Option A — Speed
    Skip denoising & deskewing → faster output → significantly lower OCR accuracy
    vs
    Option B — Quality ✓ Our Choice
    Full preprocessing pipeline → slightly slower → dramatically more accurate, especially on handwritten and noisy images ✓
    Why Quality Won: Inaccurate output misleads users. A document converter that produces wrong text is worse than no converter at all. Speed is never worth producing wrong information.
    IMPORTANCE OF ETHICAL DECISION MAKING

    Why Ethical Decisions Matter

    Every technical choice creates a ripple effect

    Impact on Users

    A student who receives wrong OCR output may submit incorrect work. Our preprocessing decision directly protects the end user from harm.

    Individual consequence of every technical choice we make

    Impact on Society

    Document digitisation tools are used in healthcare, law, and education. Errors in these contexts have consequences beyond the individual.

    Systemic effect of poor quality AI tools at scale

    System Trust

    Confidence scores and the explainability log allow users to decide whether to trust the output. Trust cannot be assumed — it must be earned.

    Long-term viability of AI tools depends on earned trust

    ETHICAL THEORIES & HUMAN-CENTERED DESIGN

    Ethical Frameworks Applied to Ocular

    Utilitarianism
    "Greatest good for the greatest number"

    Full preprocessing benefits all users, including those on slow machines and with noisy images — not just users with fast hardware.

    Deontology
    "Do the right thing, regardless of outcome"

    We never store user document images — even though doing so could improve our AI model. The rule is the rule.

    Human-Centered Design: The user controls every step — selects the file, reviews output, gives feedback. The agent assists — it does not replace.
    Addictive System Trap — Avoided: No engagement loops, no data harvesting, no dark patterns. Ocular does one job cleanly and exits.
